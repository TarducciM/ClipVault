# cookies

Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

No cookies. Neither this website nor the ClipVault app set any cookie — not technical, not analytics, not third-party. There's nothing here to consent to, so there's no banner.

Why there's no banner

A consent banner exists to ask permission for cookies that aren't strictly necessary for the site to work — analytics, advertising, tracking. Since this site sets none of those (or any cookie at all), showing a banner would suggest a kind of data processing that simply isn't happening.

What the page does load from elsewhere

Two requests leave your browser when you visit this site — neither is a cookie, but worth naming for full transparency (same disclosure as the Privacy Policy):

  • GitHub's release API, to check whether a build exists yet and show the right download links.
  • Google Fonts, to load the page's typefaces.

The desktop app

ClipVault saves your clipboard history locally on your own device — this isn't a cookie either (there's no browser involved once it's installed), and like everything else the app does, it never leaves your machine.
