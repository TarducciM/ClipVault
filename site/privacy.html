# privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

ClipVault is a local, offline desktop app: no account, no cloud, and — as far as the app and this website are concerned — nothing about you is collected, stored on a server, or sold. This page explains exactly what does and doesn't happen, in plain terms.

The desktop app

ClipVault runs entirely on your own device. It watches your system clipboard and keeps a local history of what you copy, and nothing else.

  • No accounts, no cloud sync. There's nothing to log into, and no server ClipVault talks to.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no network access at all. ClipVault makes no outbound network requests of any kind — not for updates, not for anything else. There is currently no auto-update checker; you get new versions by downloading them yourself from GitHub.
  • Everything is stored locally. Your clipboard history (text, images, and file paths) is saved in a local database on your own device, in the app's own data folder, and is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Uninstalling removes it. Since there's no account or cloud backup, uninstalling ClipVault without exporting your history first means that history is gone — same as clearing any other local app's data.

This website (the page you're reading)

This site is a single static page with no forms, no accounts, and no analytics of any kind. Two things it does load from outside your browser, both standard and both worth naming honestly:

  • GitHub's public release API — to show whether a release exists yet and, once one does, the right download link. That request goes from your browser directly to GitHub; this site never sees it.
  • Google Fonts — the page's typefaces (Sora, Inter, IBM Plex Mono) are loaded from Google's font servers, same as most websites that use Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from Google.

Neither of those is something this site collects or has access to — they're requests your own browser makes to a third party, standard for a page that shows live release info and uses web fonts.

Cookies

Neither the app nor this website use cookies. See the Cookie Policy for the full, short answer.

Changes to this page

This page is kept in sync with what the app actually does, not what it might do later — if ClipVault ever adds an opt-in feature that changes any of the above (an update checker, for instance), this page will be updated in the same change that ships it, not after the fact.
